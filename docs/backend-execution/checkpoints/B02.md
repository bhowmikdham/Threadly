## Behavior and decisions

Four owned durable tables preserve exact candidate payloads, approval binding,
action jobs and attempts independently of generation/review. Internal services
support proposal replay and pre-dispatch stopping with task-first locking and no
commits or network calls. Draft edits atomically supersede pending proposals while
preserving actions that may already be executing. PostgreSQL constraints/triggers
reject illegal transitions, mutated identities and duplicate unresolved attempts.

No external action is exposed. Held action jobs have no claim loop. B03 must build
validated exact provider payloads; B04 adds approval; B05/B06 add send/recovery.
B14/B15 are still required for Calendar actions. Outcome evidence is structurally
required by storage but not authenticated by SQL; provider semantics belong to the
future executors/reconcilers.

Deletion default: all action history blocks cascading source/account deletion.
This preserves unresolved recovery records and their sources. There is no automatic
purge. Team retention/redaction/draining policy (B19) must be settled before writes
are enabled; this temporary control does not claim to complete that product policy.

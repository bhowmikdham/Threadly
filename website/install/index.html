Early access

Bring Threadly into Gmail.

This preview runs in desktop Chrome or Microsoft Edge. You’ll sign in with your Google account from the extension.

Before you download

Google access currently requires an approved test account. Installing the extension does not add you to that list. Public signup is not yet available.

  1. Download and unzip Threadly.

    Download the early-access extension

  2. Install in your browser.

    Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder containing manifest.json.

  3. Open Gmail and sign in.

    Open Threadly from your browser’s extensions menu. Choose Sign in with Google and review the requested Gmail access.

  4. Connect Calendar when you need it.

    Open Threadly Settings, connect Google Calendar, choose your calendars, and save your timezone and working hours.

Your account, your choices.

Review the privacy information and terms before connecting. You can disconnect Google access from Threadly Settings.
